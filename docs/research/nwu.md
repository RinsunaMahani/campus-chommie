## Approximations and notes

1. **Selection programmes:** Programmes marked `selective: true` have strict quotas and early application deadlines (30 June 2026 for 2027 entry).
2. **Grade 11 vs Grade 12 Engineering requirements:** Engineering sets a conditional acceptance bar for Grade 11 (APS 31, Maths 65%, Physical Sciences 65%) and final Grade 12 threshold (APS 34, Maths 70%, Physical Sciences 70%). `min_points: 34` and subject level 6 (70%) are set as the final NSC qualification standards.
3. **Extended programmes:** NWU offers foundational/extended programmes across almost every faculty, providing educational access for learners with lower APS scores.
