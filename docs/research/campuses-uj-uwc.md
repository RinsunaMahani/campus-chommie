# UJ and UWC campuses (read 2026-10-22)

Read in a browser (Chrome) on 2026-10-22, using only the universities' own websites.

## UJ
- Source: https://www.uj.ac.za/admission-aid/assessments/maps-venues/ (page "Maps & Venues", linked from the homepage as "Directions & Maps")
- Campuses: Auckland Park Kingsway Campus (APK) (Auckland Park), Auckland Park Bunting Road Campus (APB) (Auckland Park), Doornfontein Campus (DFC) (Doornfontein), Soweto Campus (SWC) (Soweto)
  - Town/suburb: the page gives none separately. The suburb is part of each campus name, so it is repeated in brackets above.
  - The abbreviations APK, APB, DFC and SWC come from the "Safe keeping venues" list on the same page.
  - Spelling on the page varies: the campus-map heading shortens APK to "Auckland Park Campus", and one heading reads "Auckland park Bunting Road Campus" (lowercase "p").
- Quote: "Auckland Park Bunting Road Campus – A total of 55 lecture halls providing 4 412 seats. Auckland Park Kingsway Campus – A total of 66 lecture halls providing 7 787 seats. ... Doornfontein Campus – A total of 84 lecture rooms providing 5 142 seats. Soweto Campus – A total of 17 lecture halls providing 1 842 seats."
- Also checked:
  - https://www.uj.ac.za/ — has no "Campuses" link.
  - https://www.uj.ac.za/admission-aid/campus-life/ — says "4 Campuses" but doesn't name them.

## UWC
- Source: https://www.uwc.ac.za/about/uwc-at-a-glance/campus-tour (page "Campus Tour"; the campus list is in expandable sections lower down)
- Campuses:
  - Main Campus (Bellville) — "Location: Robert Sobukwe Road, Bellville 7535"
  - Faculty of Community and Health Sciences building (Bellville CBD) — "Location: 14 Blanckenberg Street, Bellville 7530" (see Problems: the page calls this a "building")
  - Tygerberg campus, Faculty of Dentistry (Parow Valley / Bellville) — "Tygerberg Hospital, Francie van Zijl Drive, Bellville 7505"; the page says it "is situated in Parow Valley"
  - Mitchell's Plain campus, Faculty of Dentistry (Mitchell's Plain) — "Melomed Mitchells Plain Private Hospital, Fourth Avenue, Mitchell's Plain 7785"
- Quote: "Main Campus Location: Robert Sobukwe Road, Bellville 7535" and "The faculty is spread across two campuses, the Tygerberg campus at Tygerberg Hospital and the Mitchell's Plain campus at Melomed Private Hospital, Mitchell's Plain."
- Also checked:
  - https://www.uwc.ac.za/contact — gives only the address "University of the Western Cape, Robert Sobukwe Road, Bellville 7535"; no campus list.
  - https://www.uwc.ac.za/about — no campus list.
