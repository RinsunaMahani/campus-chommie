# Cheap things to do near campus: research notes

Agent draft, read on 10 Oct 2026. A person checks each price against its page before release. The rule: a place goes
into a pack only when its own page states a price, or says entry is free. Third-party listings (travel sites,
blogs) don't count, even when they quote a price.

## Left out, and why

- **Javett-UP Art Centre (Hatfield):** search results quote its contact page as "UP Students: Free (with valid
  student card)", adults R85, Tuesday to Saturday 10:00-17:00. Its site couldn't be reached from our tools (the
  domain didn't resolve), so nobody has read the page. **Worth adding** once a person opens it: free for UP students.
- **Union Buildings gardens:** free according to travel sites; no official page found.
- **Pretoria Art Museum:** only old or unsourced prices found.
- **Groenkloof Nature Reserve:** only third-party prices (R35 to R45), which disagree; no City of Tshwane tariff page
  found.
