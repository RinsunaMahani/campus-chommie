# Remaining admission rules — 26 October 2026

This follow-up starts from the 19 missing records in PR #112. Three now have complete school-subject rules, two have partial rules, four have documented university-assessed routes, and ten still lack usable requirements. Partial rules and university-assessed routes do not establish eligibility.

## Complete rules

- TUT BEd Foundation Phase: APS 25, an offered Home Language at level 5, First Additional Language at level 4, Mathematics 4 or Mathematical Literacy 5, and the general English minimum of 3. The [2027 first-year guide](https://www.tut.ac.za/media/tshwane-interim/site-content/documents/First-Year-Course_Information.pdf) supplies the thresholds. The [2026 Humanities prospectus](https://www.tut.ac.za/media/tshwane-interim/site-content/images/prospectus/PART_5_Humanities_Prospectus_-2026.pdf), printed pages 126–128, identifies Sepedi, Setswana, Xitsonga and isiZulu as offered Home Languages. The programme note retains the year and asks applicants to confirm intake language availability. Missing language identity or role produces an unverified result.
- UJ Physics and Applied Mathematics (B2P77Q), and Physics and Mathematics (B2P78Q): APS 31, English 5, Mathematics 6, Physical Sciences 5. The official [Applied Mathematics course page](https://www.uj.ac.za/university-courses/bsc-in-physical-sciences-physics-and-applied-mathematics/) and [Mathematics course page](https://www.uj.ac.za/university-courses/bsc-in-physical-sciences-physics-and-mathematics/) agree with the [2026 Science yearbook](https://www.uj.ac.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/final-2026-science-apk-undergraduate-yearbook-jan2026.pdf). Its admission table and compulsory Mathematics 1A curricula resolve the conflicting lower general-prospectus footnote.

## Partial Fort Hare rules

The supplied official 2027 study guide supports these routes. Its source record remains attached to each programme.

- BEd Commerce: English 4, Life Orientation 4, Mathematics 3 with APS 27 or Mathematical Literacy 4 with APS 28, Accounting 4 plus Economics 4, and two other subjects at level 4. The alternative involving “Business management” remains unassessed because equivalence to NSC Business Studies is not established.
- BEd Science: English 4, Life Orientation 4, Mathematics 4, APS 28, one of Physical Sciences/Life Sciences/Geography at level 4 and three other subjects at level 4. The “Computer science” alternative remains unassessed because equivalence to IT/CAT is not established.

Known routes can produce `subjects_met`; Fort Hare's APS still needs manual confirmation. An unmet known option with an unresolved alternative produces `unverified`, unless another definite requirement fails.

## Published routes requiring university assessment

- Rhodes PGCE: the [official entry page](https://www.ru.ac.za/facultyofeducation/studying/pgce/index.html) requires a suitable prior degree, an undergraduate average of 60% and teaching-method subject prerequisites. It describes the 2026 intake; applicants must confirm the next intake. School marks cannot assess a degree transcript.
- Stellenbosch BA and BSc extended routes: the existing official 2026 prospectus describes faculty placement; no separate school-mark threshold is invented.
- UFS BEd extended: the existing official 2027 prospectus identifies the route without a standalone admission table. Applicants need faculty placement guidance following mainstream application.

These four return `unverified`, a source link and a specific explanation, without calculated eligibility. The readiness report lists them separately from missing and partial requirements.

## Still unresolved

Ten wholly missing records: CPUT Banking and Senior/FET BEd; MUT Community Extension; TUT Technology BEd; UFH Crop Science; UFS extended Food Systems; UJ Geography BEd and the two Geology/Physics-related routes; UMP Social Sciences. The blockers are mixed qualification routes, teaching-specialisation prerequisites, omitted thresholds or contradictory official requirements. Older or neighbouring programme requirements are not substituted.

Together with the two partial Fort Hare records, twelve still need clarification for complete automated subject assessment. Four additional routes intentionally require university assessment.
