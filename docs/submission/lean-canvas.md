# Lean Business Canvas (draft)

A draft for the Lean Business Canvas on the Sonke team page. The product facts come from what's built; **every money
figure is left for Rinsuna to decide**, marked _[decide]_, with options. Nothing here is a market statistic: where the
canvas wants one, find a citable source or leave it out.

## Problem
1. Learners can't see, in one place and in plain words, which programmes their marks meet at each university, since
   every university counts points its own way, and dates and fees are spread over 26 websites.
2. Funding (NSFAS, bursaries, loans) is found late or not at all; deadlines pass.
3. Once enrolled, a student juggles a heavy week of tests and an allowance that runs out before the next payment,
   with nobody connecting the two.

**Existing alternatives:** university websites and prospectuses; UniApplyForMe; points calculators (UniAPS, which
Rinsuna found behind a paywall with ads); WhatsApp class groups; spreadsheets or nothing for money.

## Customer segments
- **Learners in Grades 11 and 12** choosing a course (no account; often under 18).
- **First- and second-year students** at public universities, especially allowance-funded and first-generation ones.
- **Class reps**, who confirm tests and deadlines for their class.
- **Early adopters:** Grade 12 learners in the weeks applications close, and first-years in their first test season.

## Unique value proposition
**From choosing a course to graduating, in one honest app.** Your marks, your points at every public university
counted its own way, what you qualify for and how to apply today, and funding; then, once enrolled, your week and your
money connected, so a heavy test week and a late allowance are seen coming. Every fact cites its official source.

**High-level concept:** your course finder and your first-year survival kit, in one.

## Solution
1. Find my course: marks once, then points and qualifying programmes at 21 universities (more to come), grouped by
   faculty, with a three-choice plan (goal, route in, safer choice) and how to apply.
2. Funding: NSFAS and 23 other funders, what they cover, who can apply and their dates; saved funders with
   reminders.
3. My week and My money: an eight-week workload strip from class-confirmed tests, a money runway, "safe to spend",
   and alerts when both get tight, pointing to campus support.

## Channels
- Schools' Life Orientation classes and career days (the course finder needs no account).
- University first-year orientation and student representative councils; class reps inviting their class.
- WhatsApp (the bot built during the hackathon); social posts during application season.
- _[decide]_ A partner such as FNB's student offering, without linking any bank account to the app.

## Revenue streams _[decide]_
Options that keep the student side free:
- **Universities:** a paid dashboard of anonymous, aggregated trends (only where 5 or more students contribute),
  e.g. which weeks are overloaded, to plan support. Never an individual's marks or money.
- **Funders:** a paid listing or campaign tools for bursary providers, clearly labelled as such, never changing a
  learner's results.
- **A partner bank:** sponsorship of the student app, with money education content; the app never moves money or
  asks for bank details.

Put a price on each only from a real conversation or a comparable published price.

## Cost structure _[decide]_
- Hosting: free tiers now (Render, Neon, Brevo). A paid tier after the hackathon: check each provider's current
  price.
- People: development, plus checking every data pack against official sources each year (the biggest ongoing cost).
- Play Store developer account (a one-off fee: check the current amount).

## Key metrics
- Learners completing the course finder; plans made; "apply" and funding links opened before closing dates.
- Students active weekly; class reps confirming events; alerts acted on (support opened).
- Data freshness: the share of facts checked by a person this year.

## Unfair advantage
- A verified, source-cited data set for every public university's rules and dates, refreshed each year: slow to copy.
- The only app linking a student's workload and money (both owner-only), so help arrives before a crisis.
- Privacy by design: the course finder stores nothing, and marks and money are never visible to anyone else.
