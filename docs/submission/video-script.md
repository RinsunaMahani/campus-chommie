# Video Scripts: Campus Chommie

Scripts for the FNB App of the Year Hackathon 2026 submission.

## 1. 20-Second Pitch Video

**Format:** Phone screen capture at standard 60 fps; crisp text in the app's Daylight paper styling; voiceover spoken calmly and clearly (~50 words at ~2.5 words/second). No faces needed.

| Seconds | What's on screen | Words on screen | Voice line |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 – 0:05 | Fast scroll through the list of South African universities showing distinct points monograms | "26 universities. 26 ways to count points." | *"Twenty-six universities. Twenty-six ways of counting points."* |

| 0:16 – 0:20 | Clean final branding card with Campus Chommie icon and tagline | **Campus Chommie**<br>From choosing a course to graduating.<br>*Demo data: fictional student.* | *"Campus Chommie. From choosing a course to graduating."* |
