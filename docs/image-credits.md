# Image credits

Every photo in the app is a real photo from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org), used under the
licence its author chose. None is AI-made, and none shows a university's logo, crest or wordmark: each was
checked by eye, and crops cut signs out where needed. All were **cropped to 16:9 and resized** for the app
(`frontend/mobile/assets/images`). Where a page offers several licences, the one named here is the one we use.

Found by the Argon research agent on 15 Oct 2026; each Commons page re-read on 20 Oct 2026 to confirm the
file, its author and its licence. Universities without a usable photo (SMU and DUT: none on Commons; MUT, VUT,
UMP and SPU: logos in every frame) keep their text monogram.
