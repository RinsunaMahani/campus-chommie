# Image credits

Every photo in the app is a real photo from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org), used under the
licence its author chose. None is AI-made, and none shows a university's logo, crest or wordmark: each was
checked by eye, and crops cut signs out where needed. All were **cropped to 16:9 and resized** for the app
(`frontend/mobile/assets/images`): campus photos to 1600 x 900 from 1920 px copies, faculty photos to 1200 x 675.
Where a page offers several licences, the one named here is the one we use.

Found by the Argon research agent (15 and 20 Oct 2026); each Commons page re-read on 20 Oct 2026 to confirm the
file, its author and its licence. UP's and SU's first photos were replaced with sharper ones on 20 Oct 2026.
SMU has no photo on Commons and keeps its text monogram.
